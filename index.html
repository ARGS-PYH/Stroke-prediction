Precision is low: many profiles classified into the “Yes” group did not report a previous stroke. These are weighted point estimates; design-based confidence intervals have not been calculated.

What is being classified?

The target is the respondent's answer to: “Has a health professional ever told you that you had a stroke?” It is a measure of self-reported previous stroke history, not future stroke incidence.

The model uses U.S. BRFSS 2025 survey data. Its results have not been validated for Nigerian populations or clinical use.
