About the model

What the application uses and how its results were evaluated.

Outcome being classified

The target is CVDSTRK3: whether a health professional has ever told the respondent they had a stroke. This is cross-sectional, self-reported prior history — not future stroke incidence.

Model and threshold

The demonstrator uses the primary eight-feature survey-weighted Logistic Regression pipeline. Its threshold was selected using weighted Youden's J on development-only out-of-fold predictions; the held-out test set was not used to choose it.

Interpret with care

Weighted precision is low, so many positive classifications did not correspond to respondents reporting previous stroke. The returned model score is not an individual's personal medical probability.

Data & methods

Eight predictors used by the current model demonstrator.

Demographics

Age (80+ top-coded in the source) and sex.

Health history

Hypertension, heart attack / coronary heart disease history, diabetes status, and body mass index.

Lifestyle

Smoking status and leisure-time physical activity in the past 30 days.

Disclaimer

Research scope and appropriate use.

Academic demonstration only. Not for diagnosis, treatment, screening, or clinical decision-making.

The BRFSS is a U.S. population survey. These results do not establish performance in Nigeria or any other population. Some predictors may have occurred after the reported stroke, so temporal order is not established. Model explanations, external validation, design-based confidence intervals, and clinical validation have not been implemented. Use hypothetical profiles only.
